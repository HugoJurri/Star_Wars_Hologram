# Star Wars Military Technology — A Comprehensive Encyclopedia

An exhaustive guide to the weapons, vehicles, ships, and military systems of the Star Wars galaxy, spanning every major era from the Clone Wars through the rise of the Final Order.

---

## Part I: Starfighters

Starfighters are single- or dual-pilot combat spacecraft designed for dogfighting, escort, bombing, and reconnaissance. They are the backbone of tactical space combat in every era.

---

### 1.1 Republic / Clone Wars Era Starfighters

**V-19 Torrent Starfighter**
- Manufacturer: Slayn & Korpil
- Role: Space superiority fighter
- Crew: 1 clone pilot
- Armament: 2 blaster cannons, 6 concussion missiles
- Features: Folding wing design for carrier storage, capable of atmospheric and space flight
- Context: The Republic's primary early-war fighter, gradually superseded by more advanced designs as the war progressed.

**ARC-170 Starfighter (Aggressive ReConnaissance-170)**
- Manufacturer: Incom Corporation
- Role: Long-range heavy fighter / reconnaissance
- Crew: 3 (pilot, co-pilot/navigator, tail gunner) + 1 astromech droid
- Length: 14.5 meters
- Armament: 2 medium laser cannons (forward), 2 rear-facing laser cannons, 6 proton torpedoes
- Hyperdrive: Class 1.5
- Shields: Yes (deflector shields)
- Features: S-foil wings that open for combat (heat dissipation), heavy armor, long operational range
- Context: One of the most capable fighters of the Clone Wars. Its design philosophy — long-range, shielded, with a hyperdrive — directly influenced the development of the X-wing. Flown at the Battle of Coruscant and during Order 66.

**Eta-2 Actis-class Light Interceptor (Jedi Interceptor)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Systems Engineering
- Role: Jedi personal interceptor
- Crew: 1 (Jedi pilot) + 1 astromech droid (externally mounted)
- Length: 5.47 meters
- Armament: 2 laser cannons, 2 ion cannons
- Hyperdrive: None (requires external hyperspace transport ring)
- Shields: Light
- Features: Extremely agile, designed for Force-sensitive pilots who could push the craft beyond normal performance limits. Tiny cockpit, minimal life support — pure combat craft.
- Context: Flown by Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi during the Battle of Coruscant. Its predecessor, the Delta-7 Aethersprite, was used earlier in the war.

**Delta-7 Aethersprite-class Light Interceptor**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Systems Engineering
- Role: Jedi interceptor (early war)
- Crew: 1 + 1 astromech
- Armament: 2 dual laser cannons
- Hyperdrive: None (requires Syliure-31 hyperspace transport ring)
- Context: Obi-Wan's fighter during the pursuit of Jango Fett to Geonosis and Kamino.

**Z-95 Headhunter**
- Manufacturer: Incom/Subpro
- Role: Multi-role light fighter
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 2 laser cannons, concussion missiles (varies by variant)
- Context: One of the oldest and most widespread fighter designs in the galaxy. Affordable, reliable, and easy to maintain. Precursor to the X-wing. Used by Republic forces, pirates, mercenaries, and rebel cells alike.

**V-wing Starfighter (Alpha-3 Nimbus)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Systems Engineering
- Role: Fast interceptor
- Crew: 1 + 1 astromech
- Armament: 2 twin laser cannons
- Context: A late-war Republic fighter, extremely fast. Seen escorting Palpatine's shuttle. Transitioned into early Imperial service.

**Droid Starfighters (Separatist)**
- **Vulture Droid (Variable Geometry Self-Propelled Battle Droid):** Unmanned fighter that could also walk on surfaces. Mass-produced, expendable, and controlled by a central command ship. Its reliance on a control signal was a critical vulnerability — destroying the command ship disabled all linked droid fighters.
- **Tri-Fighter (Droid Tri-Fighter):** A more advanced Separatist fighter with independent processing. Three-armed design with a central eye-like sensor. More agile and dangerous than the Vulture droid.
- **Hyena-class Droid Bomber:** Bombing variant of the Vulture droid, carrying proton bombs and concussion missiles.

---

### 1.2 Imperial Starfighters

**TIE/ln Fighter (Twin Ion Engine / Line)**
- Manufacturer: Sienar Fleet Systems
- Role: Space superiority fighter
- Crew: 1
- Length: 7.24 meters
- Armament: 2 SFS L-s1 laser cannons
- Hyperdrive: None
- Shields: None
- Life support: Minimal (pilot wears sealed flight suit)
- Speed: Extremely fast and agile due to low mass
- Features: The signature hexagonal solar collector panels flanking a spherical cockpit. Mass-produced in enormous quantities. Cheap, fast, and expendable — the Imperial doctrine was quantity over quality, accepting high pilot losses.
- Context: The most produced starfighter in galactic history. Its terrifying howl (caused by ion engine acoustics in atmosphere) became the sound of Imperial power. The lack of shields and hyperdrive meant TIE pilots were effectively on suicide missions in any engagement against shielded opponents — a fact that reflects the Empire's disregard for individual lives.

**TIE/in Interceptor**
- Manufacturer: Sienar Fleet Systems
- Role: Fast interceptor
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 4 SFS L-s9.3 laser cannons (chin-mounted)
- Hyperdrive: None
- Shields: None
- Speed: Significantly faster than the standard TIE (claimed to be one of the fastest mass-produced fighters in the galaxy)
- Features: Arrow-shaped solar panels, smaller profile, improved maneuverability. Represented a partial acknowledgment that TIE pilots needed better equipment.
- Context: Introduced after the Battle of Yavin proved the standard TIE was inadequate against Rebel starfighters. Formed the elite of Imperial fighter wings, particularly with the 181st Imperial Fighter Wing led by Baron Soontir Fel.

**TIE/sa Bomber**
- Manufacturer: Sienar Fleet Systems
- Role: Tactical bomber
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 2 laser cannons, ordnance pod carrying proton bombs, thermal detonators, orbital mines, proton torpedoes, or concussion missiles
- Features: Double-hulled design — one pod for the cockpit, one for ordnance. Slow and vulnerable, relied on fighter escort.
- Context: Used for orbital bombardment, capital ship attack, and minelaying. Seen in the asteroid field pursuit in *The Empire Strikes Back*.

**TIE/d Defender**
- Manufacturer: Sienar Fleet Systems
- Role: Multi-role superiority fighter
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 4 laser cannons, 2 ion cannons, 2 warhead launchers (concussion missiles or proton torpedoes)
- Hyperdrive: Class 2
- Shields: Yes (strong deflector shields)
- Features: Three solar panel wings in a triangular configuration. The TIE Defender represented a radical departure from Imperial doctrine — it was expensive, heavily armed, shielded, and equipped with a hyperdrive. It was arguably the most capable starfighter of its era, superior to the X-wing in most respects.
- Context: Developed by Grand Admiral Thrawn as a counter to the Rebellion's qualitative fighter advantage. The project competed with (and lost political battles to) the Death Star program. Featured prominently in *Rebels*. Had the Empire invested fully in the TIE Defender instead of the Death Star, the Rebellion might have been crushed.

**TIE/sk x1 (Darth Vader's TIE Advanced x1)**
- Manufacturer: Sienar Fleet Systems
- Role: Prototype advanced fighter
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 2 heavy laser cannons, cluster missiles
- Hyperdrive: Class 4
- Shields: Yes
- Features: Bent-wing design, heavier armor than standard TIEs. A prototype that never entered mass production due to cost.
- Context: Darth Vader's personal fighter at the Battle of Yavin.

**TIE/rp Reaper**
- Role: Troop transport / gunship
- Features: Atmospheric specialist, used to deploy ground troops (including Death Troopers)
- Context: Featured in *Rogue One*, deployed at the Battle of Scarif.

### 1.3 Rebel Alliance / New Republic Starfighters

**T-65B X-wing Starfighter**
- Manufacturer: Incom Corporation
- Role: Multi-role space superiority fighter
- Crew: 1 + 1 astromech droid
- Length: 13.4 meters
- Armament: 4 KX9 laser cannons (wingtip-mounted), 2 proton torpedo launchers (3 torpedoes each)
- Hyperdrive: Class 1
- Shields: Yes (Chempat deflector shields, forward/rear adjustable)
- Speed: Good (slightly slower than a TIE Interceptor in straight-line speed, but far more versatile)
- Features: S-foils (strike foils) that lock in attack position (open) for combat and cruise position (closed) for transit. The open position increases the spread of the laser cannons and improves cooling. The astromech droid handles navigation, repairs, and system management in flight.
- Context: The symbol of the Rebel Alliance. The X-wing was originally designed for the Empire by Incom Corporation, but the entire design team defected to the Rebellion, bringing the prototypes with them. It destroyed both Death Stars — Luke Skywalker's shot at Yavin and Wedge Antilles' attack run at Endor. The perfect balance of firepower, defense, speed, and versatility.

**T-70 X-wing (Resistance era)**
- An upgraded model used by the Resistance 30 years later. Flown by Poe Dameron (Black One). Improved engines, weapons, and a more streamlined design, but the same fundamental philosophy.

**T-85 X-wing (New Republic standard)**
- The New Republic's front-line model, destroyed en masse when Starkiller Base annihilated the Hosnian system.

**BTL-A4 Y-wing Starfighter**
- Manufacturer: Koensayr Manufacturing
- Role: Bomber / fighter-bomber
- Crew: 1 pilot + 1 gunner (BTL-S3 variant) or 1 pilot only (BTL-A4 stripped version)
- Armament: 2 laser cannons, 2 ion cannons (turret-mounted in two-seat version), proton torpedo launchers, proton bombs
- Hyperdrive: Class 1
- Shields: Yes
- Features: Originally a sleek, fully-plated fighter-bomber, but the Rebellion's Y-wings were stripped of their hull plating for easier field maintenance, giving them their iconic exposed-engine look. Slow but tough.
- Context: The Rebellion's primary bomber. Used in both Death Star battles. Often assigned the dangerous task of disabling capital ships with ion cannon fire before boarding.

**RZ-1 A-wing Interceptor**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Systems Engineering (designed by General Jan Dodonna and Walex Blissex)
- Role: Fast interceptor
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 2 laser cannons, 12 concussion missiles
- Hyperdrive: Class 1
- Shields: Yes
- Speed: The fastest fighter in the Rebel fleet — possibly the fastest mass-produced fighter in the galaxy at the time
- Features: Sensor jamming equipment, wedge-shaped profile, extremely agile. Difficult to fly; only the best pilots were assigned to A-wings.
- Context: Arvel Crynyd's A-wing famously crashed into the bridge of the Super Star Destroyer *Executor* at Endor, causing it to plunge into the second Death Star. A decisive moment in the battle.

**RZ-2 A-wing (Resistance era)**
- Updated version flown by the Resistance. Piloted by Tallie Lintra. Same philosophy: speed above all.

**B-wing Starfighter (A/SF-01)**
- Manufacturer: Slayn & Korpil
- Role: Heavy assault starfighter / bomber
- Crew: 1
- Armament: 3 ion cannons, 1 heavy laser cannon, 2 auto-blasters, proton torpedoes
- Hyperdrive: Class 2
- Shields: Yes (heavy)
- Features: Unique gyroscopic cockpit that remains stable while the entire wing assembly rotates around it. Cross-shaped profile. The B-wing was the most heavily armed single-pilot starfighter in the Rebel fleet — designed specifically to take down capital ships.
- Context: Developed by the Mon Calamari engineer Quarrie with assistance from Hera Syndulla. Used at the Battle of Endor. Its unconventional design made it difficult to fly but devastatingly effective against larger targets.

**UT-60D U-wing**
- Manufacturer: Incom Corporation
- Role: Troop transport / gunship
- Crew: 2 (pilot + co-pilot/gunner) + up to 8 troops
- Armament: 2 door-mounted laser cannons
- Hyperdrive: Yes
- Features: Swing-wing design (wings fold forward for transit, sweep back for combat/landing). The Rebellion's primary tactical transport for inserting ground teams.
- Context: Featured prominently in *Rogue One* during the Battle of Scarif.

---

### 1.4 First Order / Final Order Starfighters

**TIE/fo Fighter (First Order TIE)**
- An upgraded TIE with improved solar cells, onboard life support, and marginally better survivability. Still no shields or hyperdrive in the base model. The First Order at least gave pilots ejection seats.

**TIE/sf Special Forces Fighter (Two-Seater)**
- Crew: 2 (pilot + gunner)
- Armament: Forward laser cannons + rear-facing turret, missile launcher
- Hyperdrive: Yes
- Shields: Yes
- Features: The elite variant used by First Order Special Forces. Finn and Poe escaped in one from the *Finalizer*. A significant doctrinal improvement — the First Order acknowledged that elite pilots deserved capable ships.

**TIE/wi Whisper (Modified Interceptor)**
- Kylo Ren's personal interceptor in *The Rise of Skywalker*. Equipped with stealth capabilities.

**TIE/se Striker**
- Role: Atmospheric superiority fighter
- Features: Optimized for in-atmosphere combat with aerodynamic wings. Used at the Battle of Scarif.

**TIE Dagger**
- The Final Order's Sith TIE fighter, deployed from the Xyston-class Star Destroyers at Exegol. Red-accented, aggressive design.

---

### 1.5 Mandalorian and Bounty Hunter Craft

**Kom'rk-class Fighter/Transport (Gauntlet Starfighter)**
- The signature Mandalorian multi-role craft. Rotating wings, heavy armament, troop capacity. Used by Death Watch, Bo-Katan's Nite Owls, and others.

**Fang Fighter (Mandalorian Protectorate Starfighter)**
- A supremely agile interceptor designed for Mandalorian pilots. Its rotating wing could pivot to slash at enemy craft in close quarters — designed for warriors who considered ramming an acceptable tactic.

**Firespray-31-class Patrol and Attack Craft (Slave I / Firespray)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Systems Engineering
- Crew: 1 (+ passengers/prisoners)
- Armament: Twin blaster cannons, concussion missiles, seismic charges, ion cannons, tractor beam
- Features: Unique vertical orientation — flies upright but lands horizontally. Rotating cockpit. Heavily modified by both Jango and Boba Fett with illegal weapons and systems. The seismic charges (the pulsing blue shockwave weapon) are one of the most visually and acoustically satisfying weapons in all of Star Wars.
- Context: Jango Fett's ship, inherited by Boba Fett. One of the most feared ships in the Outer Rim.

**Razor Crest**
- Manufacturer: Unknown (pre-Empire military surplus)
- Role: Gunship / transport
- Crew: 1 + passengers
- Features: Din Djarin's ship in *The Mandalorian*. A former military patrol craft converted into a bounty hunter's vessel with a carbonite freezing system for bounties, sleeping quarters, and weapon lockers. Destroyed on Tython by an Imperial orbital strike.

## Part II: Capital Ships and Fleet Vessels

Capital ships are the warships that dominate naval engagements — destroyers, cruisers, frigates, dreadnoughts, and carriers that form the backbone of galactic fleets.

---

### 2.1 Republic Navy (Clone Wars)

**Venator-class Star Destroyer**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Drive Yards
- Role: Star Destroyer / carrier hybrid
- Length: 1,137 meters
- Crew: 7,400 + 2,000 troops
- Armament: 8 heavy dual turbolaser turrets, 2 medium dual turbolaser turrets, 52 point-defense laser cannons, 4 proton torpedo launchers
- Starfighter complement: ~420 starfighters (massive ventral hangar bay with opening dorsal flight deck)
- Features: The Venator was designed as a carrier first and a battleship second — its enormous hangar could launch entire fighter wings simultaneously through its opening dorsal deck. This reflects Republic doctrine, which relied heavily on starfighter superiority supported by Jedi pilots.
- Context: The workhorse of the Republic Navy during the Clone Wars. Commanded by Jedi Generals. After the rise of the Empire, Venators were gradually retired in favor of the Imperial-class Star Destroyer, which prioritized firepower over fighter capacity.

**Acclamator-class Transgalactic Military Assault Ship**
- Role: Troop transport / assault ship
- Length: 752 meters
- Capacity: 16,000 clone troopers, 80 gunships, 48 walkers, 320 speeder bikes
- Context: The ships that delivered the clone army at the Battle of Geonosis. Purpose-built invasion transports.

**Pelta-class Frigate**
- Role: Medical frigate / supply ship
- Length: 170 meters
- Context: Lightly armed support vessels. Used extensively as medical ships and command frigates during the Clone Wars.

**Consular-class Cruiser (Republic Cruiser)**
- Role: Diplomatic vessel (converted to military use during the Clone Wars)
- Features: The red-painted diplomatic variant carried Qui-Gon and Obi-Wan to the Trade Federation blockade in *The Phantom Menace*. Military variants (Charger c70 retrofit) served as light frigates.

---

### 2.2 Separatist Navy (Clone Wars)

**Lucrehulk-class Battleship (Trade Federation Battleship)**
- Manufacturer: Hoersch-Kessel Drive
- Role: Battleship / carrier / cargo hauler
- Length: 3,170 meters (diameter)
- Armament: 185 quad turbolaser batteries, extensive point defense
- Starfighter complement: 1,500+ Vulture droids
- Features: Converted from commercial cargo haulers into warships. The distinctive ring-shaped design. The central sphere served as the command and droid control center — its destruction would disable all linked droid forces.
- Context: The ships that blockaded Naboo. Massive and heavily armed but dependent on their central control computers to coordinate droid fighters.

**Providence-class Dreadnought (Carrier/Destroyer)**
- Length: 1,088 meters
- Role: Heavy command ship
- Armament: Heavy turbolasers, ion cannons, point defense
- Context: General Grievous's flagship *Invisible Hand* was a Providence-class. Used as Separatist command ships and fleet flagships throughout the war.

**Munificent-class Star Frigate (Banking Clan Frigate)**
- Length: 825 meters
- Role: Frigate / fire support
- Armament: 2 heavy turbolaser cannons (extremely powerful bow-mounted weapons capable of threatening much larger ships), 38 point-defense cannons
- Context: Mass-produced for the Separatist fleet. The bow-mounted turbolasers gave it disproportionate punch for its size.

**Recusant-class Light Destroyer**
- Length: 1,187 meters
- Role: Light destroyer / escort
- Context: One of the most numerous Separatist warships. Cheap to produce but effective in large numbers.

---

### 2.3 Imperial Navy

**Imperial I-class Star Destroyer (ISD-I)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Drive Yards
- Role: Multi-mission capital ship (the backbone of the Imperial Navy)
- Length: 1,600 meters
- Crew: 9,235 officers, 27,850 enlisted, 9,700 stormtroopers
- Armament: 60 turbolaser batteries, 60 heavy ion cannon emplacements, 10 tractor beam projectors
- Starfighter complement: 72 TIE fighters (6 squadrons: typically 4 TIE/ln, 1 TIE Interceptor, 1 TIE Bomber)
- Ground forces carried: 20 AT-ATs, 30 AT-STs, 8 Lambda-class shuttles, 5 assault gunboats, 15 troop transports
- Shields: Dual ISD-72x deflector shield generator domes (the two "golf balls" on the bridge tower — a critical vulnerability targeted by Rebel forces)
- Hyperdrive: Class 2
- Features: The Imperial-class Star Destroyer is arguably the most iconic warship in science fiction. Its dagger-shaped silhouette was designed to project power and fear. A single ISD could subjugate an entire star system — it carried enough firepower to devastate a planet's surface from orbit and enough troops and walkers to conquer it on the ground. The Imperial doctrine of "rule through fear" was embodied in the Star Destroyer's design.
- Context: Approximately 25,000 Imperial-class Star Destroyers were built during the Empire's reign. They patrolled trade routes, enforced Imperial law, hunted Rebels, and served as mobile bases of operations. Their primary weakness was their reliance on TIE fighters that lacked shields and hyperdrives — meaning the loss of a Star Destroyer also meant the loss of all its fighters.

**Imperial II-class Star Destroyer (ISD-II)**
- An upgraded variant with heavier weapons, improved shields, and redesigned hull. Largely replaced the ISD-I in front-line service by the time of the Battle of Endor.

**Executor-class Super Star Destroyer (Dreadnought)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Drive Yards
- Role: Command ship / dreadnought
- Length: 19,000 meters (19 km)
- Crew: ~280,000+
- Armament: 5,000+ turbolaser and ion cannon emplacements
- Starfighter complement: ~5,000+
- Features: The *Executor* was Darth Vader's personal flagship and the most powerful warship in the Imperial fleet (short of the Death Stars). It was so massive that it had its own internal ecosystem of officers, crew, support staff, and even civilians. An Executor-class could single-handedly dominate a naval engagement.
- Context: Destroyed at the Battle of Endor when Arvel Crynyd's A-wing crashed into its bridge after its shield generators were knocked out, sending it plummeting into the Death Star II.

**Interdictor-class Cruiser**
- Role: Interdiction (preventing hyperspace escape)
- Length: ~600 meters
- Features: Equipped with **gravity well projectors** — four large globes that generate artificial mass shadows mimicking the gravity of a planet. These shadows prevent nearby ships from entering hyperspace, trapping them in realspace. Crucial for Imperial strategy: pull a Rebel fleet out of hyperspace into an ambush, or prevent a cornered enemy from fleeing.
- Context: Featured in *Rebels* and various engagements. A key tactical asset — without an Interdictor, fast Rebel ships could always flee to hyperspace.

**Arquitens-class Light Cruiser**
- Role: Light cruiser / patrol vessel
- Length: 325 meters
- Context: Used extensively during the Imperial era for patrol and escort duties. Featured frequently in *Rebels*.

**Raider-class Corvette**
- Role: Anti-starfighter corvette
- Length: ~150 meters
- Features: Designed specifically to counter the Rebellion's starfighter tactics. Fast, agile for its size, with weapons optimized for engaging small craft.

**Gozanti-class Cruiser (Imperial Freighter)**
- Role: Transport / light patrol
- Features: Could carry and deploy TIE fighters or AT-AT walkers externally. A ubiquitous Imperial workhorse. Seen constantly in *Rebels*.

**Lambda-class T-4a Shuttle**
- Role: Personnel shuttle
- Crew: 6 + 20 passengers
- Features: The tri-wing Imperial shuttle. Shielded, armed with laser and blaster cannons. Used by high-ranking officials — Darth Vader and Emperor Palpatine both used Lambdas.

### 2.4 Rebel Alliance / New Republic Navy

**MC80 Star Cruiser (Mon Calamari Cruiser)**
- Manufacturer: Mon Calamari Shipyards
- Role: Capital ship / fleet command
- Length: ~1,200 meters
- Features: Organic-looking, rounded hull design. Originally civilian luxury liners converted into warships. Each MC80 was unique — hand-built by Mon Calamari artisans, meaning no two had identical systems, a maintenance nightmare but also harder for the Empire to develop standardized countermeasures. Equipped with advanced shields — Mon Calamari shield technology was among the best in the galaxy.
- Context: Admiral Ackbar's flagship *Home One* was an MC80. These ships were the Rebellion's only answer to Imperial Star Destroyers. The Mon Calamari joining the Rebellion was a turning point — without their ships, the Alliance would never have had a credible fleet.

**MC75 Star Cruiser**
- A smaller Mon Calamari design. The *Profundity* (Admiral Raddus's flagship at Scarif in *Rogue One*) was an MC75.

**MC85 Star Cruiser (Resistance era)**
- The *Raddus*, General Holdo's flagship in *The Last Jedi*, was an MC85. Holdo's hyperspace ram into the *Supremacy* demonstrated the devastating potential of a capital ship at lightspeed.

**EF76 Nebulon-B Escort Frigate**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Drive Yards
- Role: Escort frigate / medical frigate
- Length: 300 meters
- Features: Distinctive "hammerhead" design with a thin connecting spar between the forward and aft sections — a known structural weakness. Originally built for the Empire, many were captured or defected to the Rebellion.
- Context: The Rebel medical frigate where Luke received his prosthetic hand at the end of *The Empire Strikes Back* was a Nebulon-B.

**CR90 Corvette (Corellian Corvette / Blockade Runner)**
- Manufacturer: Corellian Engineering Corporation
- Role: Corvette / diplomatic vessel / light combat
- Length: 150 meters
- Armament: 2 dual turbolaser turrets, 4 single turbolaser cannons
- Features: Fast, reliable, and versatile. The Tantive IV (Princess Leia's ship in the opening of *A New Hope*) was a CR90.
- Context: One of the most common warships in the Rebel fleet, used for everything from blockade running to escort duty to troop transport.

**GR-75 Medium Transport**
- Role: Cargo / troop transport
- Features: The unarmed "space whale" transports that evacuated Echo Base at Hoth, shielded by the ion cannon.

**Hammerhead Corvette (Sphyrna-class)**
- Role: Corvette / ramming ship
- Context: Famously used at the Battle of Scarif when a Hammerhead corvette rammed a disabled Imperial Star Destroyer into another, causing both to crash into the Shield Gate — one of the most celebrated tactical moments in the saga.

### 2.5 First Order Navy

**Resurgent-class Star Destroyer**
- Length: 2,915 meters (almost twice the length of an Imperial-class)
- Armament: Over 1,500 turbolasers and ion cannons
- Features: Built in secret in the Unknown Regions using Imperial-era shipyard technology. Massively more powerful than the old Imperial-class, incorporating lessons learned from the Galactic Civil War. The *Finalizer* (General Hux's ship) was a Resurgent-class.

**Mega-class Star Dreadnought (*Supremacy*)**
- Length: 13,239 meters (wing to wing: 60,542 meters)
- Role: Mobile capital / flagship / factory
- Features: Supreme Leader Snoke's flagship. Essentially a mobile city and military base — it contained factories, shipyards, training facilities, and command centers. The widest warship ever built. Crippled by Vice Admiral Holdo's hyperspace ramming maneuver.

**Mandator IV-class Siege Dreadnought (*Fulminatrix*)**
- Length: 7,669 meters
- Armament: 2 orbital autocannons (massive siege guns capable of devastating planetary targets or fleets from extreme range), 26 point-defense turbolaser cannons
- Context: The "fleet killer" that attacked the Resistance evacuation at D'Qar in *The Last Jedi*. Destroyed by Poe Dameron's bomber attack.

**Xyston-class Star Destroyer (Final Order / Sith Eternal)**
- Length: ~2,400 meters
- Armament: Axial superlaser cannon (each ship carries a planet-killing weapon derived from Death Star technology)
- Context: The Final Order fleet at Exegol — thousands of Xyston-class destroyers, each capable of destroying a planet. Represented Palpatine's ultimate escalation: an entire fleet of planet-killers. Defeated when their navigation signal was disabled and the citizens' fleet arrived.

## Part III: Superweapons

The Star Wars galaxy is plagued by a recurring pattern: massive technological terrors designed to enforce obedience through fear.

---

### 3.1 The Death Star (DS-1 Orbital Battle Station)

- Type: Space station / superweapon
- Diameter: ~120 km (later sources say 160 km)
- Crew: ~1.7 million military, ~400,000 droids
- Armament: Superlaser (planet-killing), 15,000 turbolaser batteries, 768 tractor beam emplacements, 7,000 TIE fighters
- Power source: Hypermatter reactor amplified through kyber crystals
- Superlaser mechanism: Eight tributary beams converging into one. Could destroy a planet in a single shot (Alderaan) or be fired at reduced power against fleet targets.
- Construction: Begun during the Clone Wars on Geonosis (designed by Geonosian engineers). Took approximately 19 years to complete. Construction was overseen by Orson Krennic.
- Weakness: A thermal exhaust port (2 meters wide) connected directly to the main reactor. Designed by Galen Erso as a deliberate sabotage — he was forced to work on the project and secretly engineered this vulnerability.
- Destroyed: Battle of Yavin (0 BBY). Luke Skywalker's proton torpedo shot, guided by the Force.

### 3.2 Death Star II (DS-2)

- Diameter: ~200 km (significantly larger)
- Status at destruction: Still under construction (but the superlaser was operational — a deliberate trap)
- Improvements: Eliminated the thermal exhaust port vulnerability. The superlaser could recharge fast enough to target individual capital ships, not just planets — demonstrated against Rebel cruisers at Endor.
- Destroyed: Battle of Endor (4 ABY). Lando Calrissian and Wedge Antilles flew into the superstructure and destroyed the main reactor after the shield generator on Endor's surface was destroyed by the ground team.

### 3.3 Starkiller Base

- Type: Planet converted into superweapon (the planet Ilum)
- Power source: Drained the energy of an entire star through dark energy / phantom energy harvesting
- Weapon: Fired a beam of "phantom energy" through hyperspace, capable of destroying multiple planets simultaneously across interstellar distances (destroyed the entire Hosnian system — five planets — in a single shot)
- Features: The planet's surface opened to reveal the weapon aperture. After firing, the planet was left in darkness (its sun consumed).
- Destroyed: Poe Dameron's strike team attacked a thermal oscillator, causing a chain reaction. The planet collapsed into a new star.
- Scale: Starkiller Base was vastly more powerful than either Death Star — it destroyed an entire star system from across the galaxy in a single shot.

### 3.4 Xyston Fleet (Final Order)

- Rather than a single superweapon, Palpatine's Sith Eternal built thousands of Star Destroyers each armed with a planet-killing superlaser.
- Represented the logical endpoint of superweapon doctrine: instead of one vulnerable battle station, an entire fleet of planet-killers.
- Weakness: All Xyston ships required a centralized navigation signal to leave Exegol's atmosphere. Destroying the signal source (or the command ship broadcasting it) trapped the entire fleet.

### 3.5 Other Superweapons (Canon)

**The Darksaber (weapon, not the Mandalorian lightsaber)** — A Legends superweapon (a stripped-down Death Star superlaser built into a cylindrical hull).

**Orbital bombardment (Base Delta Zero)** — Not a specific weapon but an Imperial military protocol: a fleet of Star Destroyers systematically bombarding a planet's surface until all life is destroyed and the crust is turned to slag. A slower but more conventional form of planetary destruction.

## Part IV: Ground Vehicles and Walkers

---

### 4.1 Imperial Walkers

**AT-AT (All Terrain Armored Transport)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Drive Yards
- Role: Heavy assault walker
- Height: 22.5 meters
- Length: ~20 meters
- Crew: 5 (pilot, co-pilot/gunner, 2 additional crew, commander)
- Troop capacity: 40 stormtroopers + 5 speeder bikes
- Armament: 2 heavy laser cannons (chin-mounted), 2 medium blaster cannons (temple-mounted)
- Armor: Extremely heavy (impervious to standard blaster fire and most vehicle weapons)
- Speed: ~60 km/h max
- Weaknesses: Slow, limited field of fire (head must rotate to aim), vulnerable to tripping (as demonstrated by Rebel snowspeeders using tow cables at Hoth), and vulnerable to attacks on the neck joint and underbelly.
- Context: The AT-AT is the ultimate symbol of Imperial ground power — a 20-meter-tall mechanical beast that strikes terror into defenders. Their assault on Echo Base at Hoth is one of the most iconic sequences in cinema. Their psychological impact was arguably as important as their combat capability.

**AT-ST (All Terrain Scout Transport)**
- Manufacturer: Kuat Drive Yards
- Role: Reconnaissance / anti-infantry walker
- Height: 8.6 meters
- Crew: 2 (pilot + gunner)
- Armament: 1 twin blaster cannon (chin), 1 concussion grenade launcher, 1 twin light blaster cannon
- Features: Fast, agile (for a walker), and effective against infantry and light vehicles. Vulnerable to heavy weapons and, as demonstrated on Endor, to improvised traps (Ewok log traps crushed AT-ST cockpits).
- Context: The "chicken walker" — used for scouting, patrol, and supporting AT-AT assaults. Ubiquitous in Imperial ground operations.

**AT-M6 (All Terrain MegaCaliber Six) — First Order**
- Height: ~40 meters (nearly twice the AT-AT's height)
- Armament: MegaCaliber Six turbolaser cannon (dorsal-mounted, far more powerful than AT-AT weapons), anti-aircraft missile launchers, heavy laser cannons
- Features: "Gorilla walker" design with knuckle-walking front legs for stability. Designed to resist the tow-cable tactic that toppled AT-ATs at Hoth — wider stance and heavier design made tripping virtually impossible.
- Context: Deployed at the Battle of Crait in *The Last Jedi*.

**AT-ACT (All Terrain Armored Cargo Transport)**
- A cargo variant of the AT-AT used for heavy transport. Featured at the Battle of Scarif in *Rogue One*. Taller and bulkier, with a large cargo container midsection.

**AT-TE (All Terrain Tactical Enforcer) — Republic/Clone Wars**
- Manufacturer: Rothana Heavy Engineering
- Role: Main battle walker
- Height: ~5.3 meters
- Crew: 7 (1 pilot, 1 spotter, 4 gunners, 1 mass-driver cannon operator)
- Armament: 6 anti-personnel laser cannon turrets, 1 heavy mass-driver cannon (dorsal)
- Troop capacity: 20 clone troopers
- Features: Six-legged design, lower profile than the AT-AT. More stable and versatile, capable of climbing steep surfaces (famously scaling the cliffs at the Battle of Teth). The mass-driver cannon on top could be operated as an anti-vehicle weapon.
- Context: The primary Republic walker during the Clone Wars. More practical than the AT-AT in many respects — lower profile, more legs (better stability), and a top-mounted cannon. The Empire's shift to the taller, more intimidating AT-AT reflects the transition from a republic focused on winning wars to an empire focused on projecting fear.

**AT-RT (All Terrain Recon Transport) — Republic**
- Role: Single-trooper scout walker
- Crew: 1 clone trooper (open cockpit)
- Features: Fast, agile, minimal armor. Used for reconnaissance, perimeter defense, and rapid response. Essentially a walking motorcycle.

### 4.2 Rebel/Resistance Ground Vehicles

**T-47 Airspeeder (Snowspeeder)**
- Manufacturer: Incom Corporation
- Role: Patrol / close air support (modified for cold)
- Crew: 2 (pilot + rear gunner)
- Armament: 2 laser cannons, rear harpoon gun with tow cable
- Features: Originally a civilian airspeeder adapted for military use on Hoth. Not designed for combat against armored walkers, but Rebel ingenuity (tow-cable tripping tactic) made them effective against AT-ATs.
- Context: The snowspeeder attack on AT-ATs at Hoth is one of the defining tactical engagements of the saga.

**Rebel Troop Transports:** The Rebellion had limited ground vehicles and relied heavily on captured or improvised equipment, infantry, and air support.

**Hailfire Droid (IG-227 Hailfire-class Droid Tank)**

**NR-N99 Persuader-class Droid Enforcer (Tank Droid)**

**STAP (Single Trooper Aerial Platform)**

## Part V: Infantry Weapons and Personal Equipment

---

### 5.1 Blasters

**E-11 Blaster Rifle (Stormtrooper Standard)**
- Manufacturer: BlasTech Industries
- Type: Blaster rifle
- Range: ~300 meters effective
- Features: The iconic stormtrooper weapon. Compact, reliable, adjustable power settings. Based on the real-world Sterling submachine gun (the film prop was literally a modified Sterling).

**DC-15A Blaster Rifle (Clone Trooper Standard)**
- The Clone Wars-era standard rifle. Longer range and more powerful than the E-11. Clone troopers also used the shorter DC-15S blaster carbine.

**DL-44 Heavy Blaster Pistol**
- Han Solo's weapon of choice. Powerful for a pistol, customized with a scope. Real-world prop based on the Mauser C96 pistol.

**ELG-3A Blaster Pistol**
- A compact, elegant blaster often used by diplomats and dignitaries. Padmé Amidala's weapon.

**SE-14r Light Repeating Blaster**
- An Imperial officer's sidearm capable of semi-automatic or fully automatic fire.

**DLT-19 Heavy Blaster Rifle**
- A stormtrooper heavy weapon, used for suppression fire and long-range engagement. Prop based on the MG-34 machine gun.

**E-5 Blaster Rifle (Battle Droid Standard)**
- The standard weapon of B1 battle droids. Cheap, mass-produced, and of mediocre accuracy — matching its wielders.

**Bowcaster (Wookiee)**
- A magnetic accelerator weapon that fires explosive energy quarrels (plasma-wrapped projectiles). Chewbacca's signature weapon. Far more powerful than standard blasters — the quarrel fired by Han in *The Force Awakens* sent stormtroopers flying.

**Cycler Rifle**
- A primitive slug-throwing rifle used by Tusken Raiders. Fires solid projectiles rather than plasma bolts, making it effective against shielded targets (shields optimized for blaster bolts may not stop physical slugs).

**Disruptor Rifle/Pistol**
- An extremely illegal weapon that breaks down matter at the molecular level. Causes agonizing death. Banned by both the Republic and the Empire under treaty. Used by criminal elements and occasionally by Imperial agents who operated outside official rules.

### 5.2 Grenades and Explosives

**Thermal Detonator**
- A spherical explosive capable of vaporizing everything within a significant radius. Extremely powerful — Princess Leia (disguised as Boushh) threatened Jabba with one.

**Droid Popper (EMP Grenade)**
- An electromagnetic pulse grenade that disables droids and electronics. Used extensively during the Clone Wars.

**Proton Charge**
- Demolitions explosive used for breaching fortifications and sabotage.

**Det Charge**
- Standard military explosive charge used for demolitions.

### 5.3 Melee Weapons

**Electrostaff (MagnaGuard)**
- Wielded by General Grievous's IG-100 MagnaGuard droids. Electrified staff capable of blocking lightsaber strikes (made from phrik alloy, resistant to lightsabers).

**Vibroblade / Vibrosword**
- A blade weapon that vibrates at extreme frequencies, increasing cutting power. Some vibroblades use cortosis or phrik alloys to resist lightsabers.

**Z6 Riot Control Baton**
- A stormtrooper melee weapon, electrified. "Traitor!" — the FN-2199 stormtrooper wielded one against Finn.

**Force Pike**
- Used by the Emperor's Royal Guard. An electrified polearm.

**Beskar Spear**
- A pure beskar melee weapon, lightsaber-resistant. Used by Din Djarin before being reforged.

## Part VI: Armor and Protective Equipment

---

### 6.1 Clone Trooper Armor (Phase I and Phase II)

**Phase I Clone Trooper Armor**
- Used during the early Clone Wars. Based on Mandalorian armor design (fitting, given the clones were based on Jango Fett). Heavier, less comfortable, with a distinctive T-visor helmet. Environmental sealing for vacuum and hostile atmospheres.

**Phase II Clone Trooper Armor**
- Introduced midway through the Clone Wars. Lighter, more comfortable, with improved life support, HUD systems, and modular attachments. Allowed more individual customization — many units painted their armor with distinctive colors and markings reflecting their legion and battle honors.

**Clone Specialist Variants:**
- **ARC Troopers:** Elite troops with enhanced armor, kamas (skirt-like armor), and additional weapons.
- **Clone Commandos (Republic Commando):** Katarn-class armor — heavier, with advanced HUD, shields, and stealth capabilities. Operated in four-person squads.
- **Clone Paratroopers:** Jump-capable variants with stabilization equipment.
- **Galactic Marines:** Cold-weather and harsh-environment specialists.
- **Scout Troopers (BARC Troopers):** Lighter armor optimized for recon and speed.

### 6.2 Stormtrooper Armor

- Manufacturer: Various (primarily derived from clone armor design)
- Material: Plastoid composite over a body glove
- Protection: Effective against shrapnel, glancing blaster shots, environmental hazards (sealed for brief vacuum exposure). Does *not* reliably stop a direct blaster bolt — stormtroopers hit by blaster fire typically go down. The armor does distribute energy from impacts, potentially keeping the trooper alive (unconscious, not dead) in some cases.
- HUD: Helmet contains communication systems, basic HUD, filtration, and limited environmental data. Famously restricts peripheral vision — "I can't see a thing in this helmet!" (Luke Skywalker).
- Variants:
  - **Sandtrooper:** Desert variant with cooling systems and survival gear.
  - **Snowtrooper:** Cold-weather variant with insulated body glove and heated armor.
  - **Scout Trooper:** Lightweight armor for speeder bike recon.
  - **Shoretrooper:** Coastal/tropical variant (seen on Scarif).
  - **Death Trooper:** Elite variant — black armor with advanced encryption, enhanced sensors, and scrambled voice communications. Served as bodyguards for high-ranking officers (Director Krennic).
  - **Purge Trooper:** Specialized for anti-Jedi operations. Equipped with electrostaffs and electrohammers.
  - **Dark Trooper:** Advanced combat droids (or droid-armored troopers). Third-generation Dark Troopers were fully autonomous combat droids with extreme durability — seen in *The Mandalorian*.
  - **Range Trooper:** Heavy cold-weather/hostile-environment trooper with magnetic boots (seen on the conveyex train in *Solo*).
  - **Incinerator Trooper:** Flamethrower-equipped trooper.

### 6.3 Mandalorian Armor (Beskar'gam)

- Material: **Beskar** (Mandalorian iron) — one of the strongest materials in the galaxy. Capable of resisting lightsaber strikes, blaster bolts, and extreme impacts.
- Features: Modular design. Helmet with advanced HUD, thermal imaging, and comms. Often equipped with jetpacks (Z-6 jet pack), wrist-mounted weapons (flamethrower, whipcord launcher, whistling birds — guided micro-munitions), and vambraces (forearm-mounted gadgets).
- Cultural significance: Armor is central to Mandalorian identity. "This is the Way." A Mandalorian's armor is sacred — beskar is passed down through generations and clans. Removing one's helmet is prohibited in some sects (the Children of the Watch) but not in mainstream Mandalorian culture.
- Context: Din Djarin's armor, forged from an ingot of beskar, proved resistant to blaster fire, flamethrowers, and even a lightsaber.

### 6.4 Other Notable Armor

- **Royal Guard Armor:** Red-robed armor of the Emperor's personal guard. Ceremonial in appearance but highly protective.
- **Praetorian Guard Armor:** Supreme Leader Snoke's personal guard in *The Last Jedi*. Red armor, various melee weapons.
- **Senate Guard / Blue Guard:** Republic-era guards in blue armor.
- **Rebel/Resistance troopers:** Relatively light armor — helmets and vests. The Rebellion couldn't afford standardized heavy armor.

## Part VII: Specialized Military Systems

---

### 7.1 Planetary Defense Systems

**Planetary Shield Generator**
- A ground-based installation projecting an energy shield over an entire planet or region. The shield generator on Endor protected the second Death Star. The shield gate over Scarif blocked orbital access to the planet. Destroying the generator was always the critical objective.

**Ion Cannon (v-150 Planet Defender)**
- The Rebel ion cannon at Echo Base disabled Imperial Star Destroyers in orbit, allowing transports to escape. Ion weapons disable electronics and systems rather than causing physical destruction — a disabled Star Destroyer would drift helplessly until systems were restored.

**Turbolaser Towers**
- Ground-based heavy turbolaser emplacements for orbital defense. The surface of Scarif was dotted with them.

### 7.2 Mines and Area Denial

**Proximity Mines**
- Deployed in asteroid fields and around installations.

**Conner Net**
- An electromagnetic net that disables ship systems. Used by bounty hunters and pirates.

**Seismic Charges**
- Jango Fett's weapon of choice in the Geonosis asteroid chase. Creates a devastating planar shockwave that obliterates everything in its expanding ring. Visually and aurally one of the most memorable weapons in Star Wars — the brief silence before the detonation "BWONNNG" is legendary sound design.

### 7.3 Orbital and Ship-to-Surface Weapons

**Turbolasers**
- The primary capital ship weapon. Essentially scaled-up blaster technology — high-powered plasma bolts fired from massive cannons. A single turbolaser bolt from a Star Destroyer carries enough energy to devastate city blocks.

**Ion Cannons (Ship-mounted)**
- Disable enemy ships' systems without destroying them. Used when the objective is capture rather than destruction.

**Proton Torpedoes**
- Guided energy torpedoes used by starfighters and capital ships. They carry a proton-scattering energy warhead that is particularly effective against shielded targets. Luke used proton torpedoes to destroy the Death Star.

**Concussion Missiles**
- A kinetic/energy warhead missile, used widely by fighters and capital ships.

**Orbital Bombardment**
- Star Destroyers could devastate planetary surfaces from orbit — a process the Empire formalized as "Base Delta Zero" (complete surface destruction).

### 7.4 Electronic and Information Warfare

**Sensor Jamming**
- Both A-wings and some capital ships carried sensor jamming equipment to disrupt enemy targeting and communications.

**Code-Breaking and Slicing**
- "Slicing" is the Star Wars equivalent of hacking. Astromech droids (R2-D2) were experts at infiltrating enemy computer systems, opening doors, disabling traps, and extracting data.

**HoloNet**
- The galaxy-wide communication network. Controlling the HoloNet was a key strategic asset — the Empire censored and controlled it, limiting Rebel communication.

**Probe Droids (Viper Probe Droid)**
- Deployed by Star Destroyers across thousands of star systems to search for Rebel bases. Self-destructing when discovered, they transmitted location data before detonation. One found Echo Base on Hoth.

## Part VIII: Military Doctrine and Strategy

---

### 8.1 The Tarkin Doctrine (Imperial Strategy)

Grand Moff Wilhuff Tarkin authored the foundational Imperial military doctrine: **rule through the fear of force rather than force itself.** The Death Star was the ultimate expression of this — a weapon so terrifying that no system would dare rebel. The doctrine prioritized overwhelming military superiority, centralized command, and the willingness to make examples of dissent (Alderaan's destruction was intended as a demonstration, not a military necessity).

**Weaknesses of the doctrine:** It created a dependence on superweapons and top-down command structures. When the Death Star was destroyed, the "fear" evaporated and systems flocked to the Rebellion. The Empire was less an adaptive military and more a terror state — when the instruments of terror were destroyed, the system collapsed.

### 8.2 Rebel Alliance Strategy

The Rebellion operated as a classic asymmetric warfare force — outgunned, outnumbered, but more agile, more motivated, and fighting on the defensive (strategically) while conducting hit-and-run offensives (tactically).

**Key principles:**
- Intelligence superiority (Bothan spynet, stolen plans, embedded agents like Cassian Andor)
- Avoid pitched fleet battles (until absolutely necessary — Scarif, Endor)
- Strike high-value targets (superweapons, supply lines, key installations)
- Maintain distributed, redundant command (losing one base doesn't end the war)
- Leverage starfighter superiority (X-wings vs. TIEs favored the Rebellion in quality)
- Inspire popular support (every victory was propaganda)

### 8.3 Clone Wars Grand Strategy

The Clone Wars were unique because both sides were controlled by the same person (Palpatine/Sidious). The war's purpose was never to be won by either side — it was to:
1. Justify the creation of a massive military (the clone army)
2. Expand Palpatine's emergency executive powers
3. Spread the Jedi thin across the galaxy, far from Coruscant
4. Erode public trust in the Jedi and the Senate
5. Provide the pretext for Order 66 and the declaration of Empire

Militarily, the Republic's advantage was its clone troopers (superior individual quality) and Jedi generals (Force-enhanced leadership). The Separatists' advantage was industrial capacity (endless droid production) and decentralization.

### 8.4 First Order Doctrine

The First Order combined Imperial fear-based doctrine with fanatical ideological indoctrination. Unlike the Empire, which relied on volunteers, conscripts, and mercenaries, the First Order kidnapped children and raised them from birth as soldiers (Finn's backstory). This created a more zealous but ultimately more brittle military — soldiers who had never been given a choice were the most likely to defect when shown an alternative.
