# The Star Wars Universe — A Comprehensive Guide

---

## 1. The Galaxy: Structure and Geography

The entire Star Wars saga takes place within a single spiral galaxy, roughly 100,000 light-years across, containing over 400 billion stars. It is divided into concentric regions radiating outward from a supermassive black hole at the center. Travel between systems is made possible by **hyperspace**, an alternate dimension that allows faster-than-light travel along mapped routes called **hyperspace lanes**. The most important of these are the Perlemian Trade Route, the Corellian Run, the Hydian Way, and the Rimma Trade Route — ancient highways that shaped galactic civilization.

### 1.1 Galactic Regions

**The Deep Core**
The innermost region, densely packed with stars and gravitational anomalies. Navigation here is extremely dangerous. It has served as a hiding place for Sith lords and secret Imperial installations like Byss.

**The Core Worlds**
The political, economic, and cultural heart of the galaxy. Home to the galaxy's wealthiest and most influential planets, including Coruscant (the galactic capital for most of recorded history), Corellia (a legendary shipbuilding world), and Alderaan (a peaceful, cultured planet destroyed by the Death Star). Core World citizens often view themselves as the pinnacle of galactic civilization.

**The Colonies**
A ring of worlds settled early in galactic expansion. These are prosperous, well-developed systems that serve as an economic bridge between the Core and the Mid Rim.

**The Inner Rim**
A transitional zone between the wealthy Core-adjacent regions and the more rugged Mid Rim. It was once the frontier of galactic civilization before expansion pushed outward.

**The Mid Rim**
A vast band of moderately developed worlds. This is where much of the galaxy's "middle class" lives — planets like Naboo (home of Padmé Amidala and Palpatine) and Kashyyyk (the Wookiee homeworld). Politically diverse and economically varied.

**The Outer Rim Territories**
The largest and most sparsely populated region. The Outer Rim is famous for lawlessness, independence, and harsh frontier life. It is home to Tatooine, Mustafar, Hoth, Geonosis, and many other iconic planets. The Hutt crime families dominate much of the Outer Rim, and the Republic's authority here was always tenuous — which made it fertile ground for Separatist and later Rebel sympathies.

**Wild Space**
Beyond the Outer Rim lies Wild Space — partially explored but not integrated into galactic civilization. The Chiss Ascendancy (home of Grand Admiral Thrawn) exists here.

**The Unknown Regions**
The largely uncharted western quadrant of the galaxy. Hyperspace travel here is extremely difficult due to anomalies. The Unknown Regions play a critical role in the sequel trilogy: this is where Palpatine's Sith Eternal cult secretly built the Final Order fleet on the hidden world of Exegol, and where the First Order retreated and rebuilt after the Empire's fall.

## 2. Planets and Environments

Star Wars is known for its "single-biome" planets — worlds defined by one dominant environment. Below is a selection of the most important worlds, organized by type.

### 2.1 Desert Worlds
- **Tatooine** — A harsh, twin-sunned desert world in the Outer Rim. Controlled by Hutt gangsters. Home to Anakin and Luke Skywalker. Despite its insignificance on the galactic stage, it is one of the most pivotal locations in the saga.
- **Jakku** — Another desert planet, site of a massive Imperial–Rebel battle after Endor. Home of Rey before her journey began.
- **Jedha** — A cold desert moon sacred to followers of the Force. The Empire mined it for kyber crystals to power the Death Star's superlaser.
- **Geonosis** — An arid, rocky world home to insectoid Geonosians. Site of the first battle of the Clone Wars and the secret construction site of the Death Star.

### 2.2 Forest and Jungle Worlds
- **Endor (Forest Moon of)** — A lush forest moon inhabited by Ewoks. Site of the decisive Battle of Endor and the death of Emperor Palpatine (originally).
- **Kashyyyk** — A jungle world dominated by massive wroshyr trees. Homeworld of the Wookiees.
- **Yavin 4** — A jungle moon that housed the main Rebel Alliance base during the Battle of Yavin (destruction of the first Death Star).
- **Dagobah** — A swampy, fog-covered world where Yoda lived in exile and where Luke trained.
- **Felucia** — A vibrant, bioluminescent jungle world.
- **Ajan Kloss** — Dense jungle world used as a Resistance base in the sequel era.

### 2.3 Ice and Snow Worlds
- **Hoth** — A frozen wasteland that served as the Rebel Alliance's Echo Base. Site of one of the saga's most iconic battles.
- **Ilum** — A frigid, sacred Jedi world where younglings harvested kyber crystals. The First Order later hollowed it out to build Starkiller Base.

### 2.4 Urban/City Worlds
- **Coruscant** — An entire planet covered by a single, planet-spanning city. Capital of the Republic, then the Empire. Seat of the Jedi Temple and the Galactic Senate. Coruscant is the political and symbolic center of the galaxy, with a population in the trillions and city levels stacked kilometers deep, from glittering upper levels to the lawless underworld below.
- **Nar Shaddaa** — The "Smuggler's Moon," orbiting Nal Hutta. A grimy, neon-lit ecumenopolis (city-planet) controlled by the Hutts, full of crime, bounty hunters, and black markets.

### 2.5 Volcanic and Lava Worlds
- **Mustafar** — A hellish volcanic world. Site of the climactic duel between Obi-Wan Kenobi and Anakin Skywalker, where Anakin was burned and became Darth Vader. Later home to Vader's fortress.
- **Sullust** — A volcanic world with underground cities, allied with the Rebellion.

### 2.6 Ocean and Water Worlds
- **Kamino** — An ocean world of endless rain and towering stilt-cities. Home of the cloners who created the Republic's clone army.
- **Mon Cala (Mon Calamari)** — An ocean world home to two sentient species, the Mon Calamari and the Quarren. The Mon Calamari became crucial Rebel allies, building many of the Rebellion's capital ships.
- **Ahch-To** — A remote oceanic world with rocky islands. Site of the first Jedi Temple, and where Luke Skywalker lived in self-imposed exile.
- **Scarif** — A tropical, ocean-covered world with an Imperial data vault. Site of the Battle of Scarif in *Rogue One*.

### 2.7 Other Notable Worlds
- **Naboo** — A verdant, idyllic world with rolling grasslands, elegant cities, and a vast underwater network of caves. Homeworld of Padmé Amidala and Sheev Palpatine. Also home to the Gungans.
- **Mandalore** — A war-scarred world, once lush, now largely covered in white desert due to centuries of warfare. Homeworld and cultural center of the Mandalorians.
- **Bespin** — A gas giant with Cloud City, a floating mining colony run by Lando Calrissian. Famous as the site of the "I am your father" reveal.
- **Lothal** — An Outer Rim world featured in *Rebels*, with grasslands and a Jedi Temple.
- **Dathomir** — A dark, misty world home to the Nightsisters, Force-wielding witches, and the Nightbrothers (Darth Maul's people).
- **Exegol** — A hidden Sith world deep in the Unknown Regions. Dark, lightning-scarred, and home to the Sith Eternal.
- **Crait** — A mineral world with a white salt surface over red crystal. Site of the Resistance's last stand in *The Last Jedi*.

## 3. Species of the Galaxy

The Star Wars galaxy teems with sentient and non-sentient life. Thousands of sentient species are represented in the Galactic Senate and populate the galaxy's worlds.

### 3.1 Major Sentient Species

**Humans**
The most numerous and politically dominant species in the galaxy. Humans originated on Coruscant (according to most legends) and colonized the galaxy extensively. Most major characters — Skywalkers, Palpatine, Solo — are human. The Empire was notoriously human-supremacist (*High Human Culture*), marginalizing and enslaving non-human species.

**Twi'leks**
Humanoids with colorful skin and two head-tails (lekku) used for communication. Native to Ryloth. Twi'leks are widespread across the galaxy, often as dancers, diplomats, or — tragically — as victims of the slave trade due to their appearance.

**Wookiees**
Towering, fur-covered humanoids from Kashyyyk, known for immense physical strength, fierce loyalty, and long lifespans (several hundred years). They speak Shyriiwook, a language of growls and roars. Chewbacca is the most famous Wookiee. The Empire enslaved many Wookiees.

**Rodians**
Green-skinned, bug-eyed humanoids from Rodia. Known as hunters. Greedo, who confronted Han Solo in the Mos Eisley Cantina, is a Rodian.

**Togruta**
Humanoids with distinctive horn-like montrals and head-tails, native to Shili. Ahsoka Tano and Shaak Ti are Togruta. They have a natural ability to sense their surroundings through echolocation via their montrals.

**Zabrak**
Horned humanoids from Iridonia (and the subspecies of Dathomirians from Dathomir). Darth Maul is a Dathomirian Zabrak Nightbrother.

**Mon Calamari**
Amphibious, fish-like humanoids from Mon Cala. Renowned shipbuilders and military strategists. Admiral Ackbar is the most well-known Mon Calamari.

**Trandoshans**
Reptilian humanoids who worship a deity called the Scorekeeper. They are natural rivals of the Wookiees and often work as bounty hunters (Bossk).

**Duros**
Blue-green-skinned, noseless humanoids who were among the earliest species to develop hyperspace travel. They are legendary navigators and pilots. Cad Bane is a Duros.

**Chiss**
Blue-skinned, red-eyed near-humans from the Unknown Regions. They govern the Chiss Ascendancy, a secretive and highly disciplined civilization. Grand Admiral Thrawn is the most famous Chiss.

**Hutts**
Massive, slug-like beings native to Nal Hutta. Despite their appearance, Hutts are intelligent, long-lived (up to 1,000 years), and extremely powerful as crime lords. The Hutt Cartel controls vast swathes of the Outer Rim. Jabba the Hutt is the most iconic.

**Gungans**
Amphibious beings native to Naboo's underwater regions. They live in bubble-like cities beneath the planet's lakes and seas. Jar Jar Binks and Boss Nass are Gungans. They possess unique plasma-based technology.

**Bothans**
Fur-covered humanoids known for their intelligence network. "Many Bothans died to bring us this information" — they were pivotal spies for the Rebel Alliance.

**Jawas**
Small, hooded scavengers native to Tatooine. They roam the deserts in massive sandcrawlers, collecting and trading salvaged droids and technology.

**Tusken Raiders (Sand People)**
Nomadic, warrior people of Tatooine who wrap themselves in cloth and breathe through filtration masks. Their culture is deeply tied to the desert, and they are far more complex and honorable than outsiders typically assume — a theme explored deeply in *The Book of Boba Fett*.

**Ewoks**
Small, bear-like forest dwellers of Endor's moon. Despite their primitive technology (stone weapons, gliders), they proved decisive in the Battle of Endor.

### 3.2 Other Notable Species

- **Cereans** — Large-brained humanoids (Ki-Adi-Mundi)
- **Nautolans** — Amphibious, tentacle-headed species (Kit Fisto)
- **Kel Dor** — Mask-wearing species who breathe unusual atmospheres (Plo Koon)
- **Mirialans** — Near-humans with geometric facial tattoos (Luminara Unduli, Barriss Offee)
- **Ithorians** — Gentle, "hammerhead" herbivores known as environmental stewards
- **Weequay** — Tough, leathery-skinned humanoids often seen as pirates or enforcers (Hondo Ohnaka)
- **Talz** — Furry, four-eyed beings from Orto Plutonia
- **Kaminoans** — Tall, slender, pale beings who mastered cloning technology
- **Geonosians** — Insectoid beings who built the Death Star plans in their hive factories
- **Pau'ans** — Tall, gaunt humanoids with lined faces (the Grand Inquisitor)

### 3.3 Notable Non-Sentient Creatures
- **Rancor** — Massive predators, one kept by Jabba the Hutt
- **Sarlacc** — A pit-dwelling creature that digests prey over thousands of years (the Great Pit of Carkoon)
- **Krayt Dragon** — Enormous desert predators on Tatooine; their pearls are extremely valuable
- **Mynock** — Parasitic, bat-like silicon creatures that feed on energy
- **Tauntaun** — Furry, lizard-like mounts used on Hoth
- **Wampa** — Predatory ice creatures on Hoth
- **Purrgil** — Space-dwelling whale-like creatures that naturally travel through hyperspace; they inspired the invention of hyperspace travel
- **Loth-Wolf** — Mystical, Force-connected wolves of Lothal
- **Porg** — Small seabirds native to Ahch-To
- **Bantha** — Massive, woolly beasts of burden, common on Tatooine

## 4. The Force

The Force is the metaphysical foundation of the Star Wars universe — an energy field generated by all living things that binds the galaxy together. It is at once a spiritual concept, a natural phenomenon, and a source of extraordinary power.

### 4.1 Nature of the Force

The Force exists in all living things through **midi-chlorians**, microscopic organisms that live symbiotically within the cells of all life. A being's midi-chlorian count correlates with their Force sensitivity (Anakin Skywalker's count was over 20,000 — the highest ever recorded). The Force has two primary aspects:

- **The Living Force** — Connected to the present moment, to instinct, to the life energy of all beings. Qui-Gon Jinn was a strong advocate of the Living Force.
- **The Cosmic Force** — Connected to destiny, prophecy, and the deeper patterns of the universe. It feeds on the energy of the Living Force and binds the galaxy together across space and time.

### 4.2 The Light Side and the Dark Side

The Force has a natural state of balance, but it can be channeled through two philosophical orientations:

**The Light Side** — Associated with peace, serenity, compassion, selflessness, and harmony with the natural flow of the Force. Light-side users (primarily the Jedi) seek to let the Force guide them rather than bending it to their will.

**The Dark Side** — Fueled by passion, anger, fear, hatred, and the desire for power. Dark-side users (primarily the Sith) impose their will on the Force to gain strength. The dark side is described as quicker and more seductive, but it corrupts its users — physically and spiritually.

### 4.3 Force Abilities

**Common abilities:**
- Telekinesis (push, pull, choke, crush)
- Enhanced reflexes and precognition
- Mind tricks (influencing weak-willed beings)
- Force sense (detecting danger, emotions, presences)
- Force jump and speed

**Advanced light-side abilities:**
- Force healing
- Force projection (projecting one's image across the galaxy — used by Luke on Crait)
- Becoming a Force ghost after death (discovered by Qui-Gon, perfected by Yoda, Obi-Wan, Anakin)
- Battle meditation (bolstering allies)

**Dark-side abilities:**
- Force lightning (a signature Sith power)
- Force drain (siphoning life energy)
- Essence transfer (transferring one's consciousness — Palpatine's secret to survival)
- Sith alchemy and sorcery
- Force rage

**The Force Dyad** — An extremely rare bond between two beings, making them essentially one in the Force. Rey and Ben Solo shared a dyad — a connection that allowed them to see, touch, and transfer objects across vast distances.

### 4.4 Prophecy and Balance

A central prophecy states that a "Chosen One" will bring balance to the Force. Anakin Skywalker was believed to be that Chosen One — born of no father, created by the Force itself (or possibly by Sith manipulation of midi-chlorians). His path from Jedi prodigy to Sith Lord and back to redemption is the backbone of the entire saga.

## 5. Factions and Governments

### 5.1 The Galactic Republic (c. 25,000 BBY – 19 BBY)

A democratic federation that governed the galaxy for roughly 25,000 years. Its legislature was the **Galactic Senate**, based on Coruscant, with representatives from thousands of worlds. The Republic was served and protected by the **Jedi Order**, who acted as peacekeepers, diplomats, and generals.

By its final centuries, the Republic had become bloated, corrupt, and ineffective — a weakness Palpatine exploited masterfully. The Clone Wars (22–19 BBY) were engineered by Palpatine from both sides: he controlled the Separatist movement through Count Dooku while serving as Supreme Chancellor of the Republic. When Order 66 was executed, the Jedi were destroyed, and Palpatine declared himself Emperor.

### 5.2 The Jedi Order

An ancient monastic order of Force-sensitive beings dedicated to the light side of the Force. Founded thousands of years before the saga, the Jedi were headquartered in the **Jedi Temple** on Coruscant. Key principles include emotional detachment, selfless service, and the rejection of personal attachment (a rule that directly contributed to Anakin's fall).

**Structure:**
- *Younglings* — Children brought to the Temple for training
- *Padawans* — Apprentices assigned to a Jedi Knight or Master
- *Jedi Knights* — Full members who have passed the Trials
- *Jedi Masters* — Experienced Knights recognized for wisdom
- *The Jedi Council* — A governing body of 12 Masters (including Yoda, Mace Windu, Ki-Adi-Mundi)
- *The Grand Master* — The Council's leader (Yoda held this role for centuries)

The Jedi wielded **lightsabers** — plasma blades powered by kyber crystals. Blade color is determined by the kyber crystal's bond with its wielder: blue and green are most common for Jedi, purple was unique to Mace Windu, and white crystals are purified from red Sith crystals (Ahsoka's blades).

### 5.3 The Sith Order

An ancient order of dark-side Force users, the sworn enemies of the Jedi. Originally the Sith were a species on the planet Korriban (Moraband), but the term came to refer to the dark-side tradition. For most of their history, the Sith destroyed themselves through infighting — until **Darth Bane** established the **Rule of Two**: only two Sith at a time, a Master and an Apprentice. This allowed the Sith to operate in secret for a thousand years, culminating in Palpatine's takeover of the galaxy.

**Notable Sith Lords:**
- **Darth Bane** — Created the Rule of Two
- **Darth Plagueis** — Palpatine's master, who could manipulate midi-chlorians to create life and prevent death
- **Darth Sidious (Palpatine)** — The saga's ultimate villain, the most powerful Sith Lord, who destroyed the Republic from within
- **Darth Tyranus (Count Dooku)** — A former Jedi, Palpatine's apprentice during the Clone Wars
- **Darth Maul** — Palpatine's first apprentice, a Dathomirian Zabrak warrior
- **Darth Vader (Anakin Skywalker)** — The Chosen One, turned to the dark side, eventually redeemed

### 5.4 The Galactic Empire (19 BBY – 5 ABY)

Born from the ashes of the Republic when Palpatine declared himself Emperor. The Empire ruled through fear, military force, and the suppression of all dissent. Its philosophy was built on human supremacy, authoritarian control, and the doctrine of overwhelming military power (embodied by the Death Star project — a battle station capable of destroying entire planets).

**Key Imperial elements:**
- **Stormtroopers** — The Empire's primary infantry, recruited (not cloned) soldiers in white armor
- **Star Destroyers** — Massive, wedge-shaped warships that served as the backbone of the Imperial Navy
- **The Death Star** — A moon-sized battle station with a planet-killing superlaser (two were built)
- **The Inquisitorius** — Dark-side agents trained to hunt surviving Jedi after Order 66
- **The ISB (Imperial Security Bureau)** — The Empire's secret police and intelligence agency
- **Moffs and Grand Moffs** — Regional military governors (Grand Moff Tarkin governed the Outer Rim)

### 5.5 The Rebel Alliance (c. 5 BBY – 5 ABY)

A coalition of resistance cells, dissident senators, and freedom fighters that rose to oppose the Empire. Key founders included Bail Organa (Alderaan), Mon Mothma (Chandrila), and Ahsoka Tano (operating covertly). The Rebellion was always massively outgunned and relied on guerrilla tactics, stolen intelligence, and the courage of individual heroes.

**Key victories:**
- The Battle of Scarif — Stealing the Death Star plans (*Rogue One*)
- The Battle of Yavin — Destroying the first Death Star (Luke Skywalker's shot)
- The Battle of Endor — Destroying the second Death Star and killing the Emperor

After Endor, the Rebel Alliance reorganized as the **New Republic**.

### 5.6 The New Republic (5 ABY – 34 ABY)

Established after the Empire's defeat, the New Republic relocated the capital on a rotating basis among member worlds (to prevent another Coruscant-style concentration of power). It demilitarized significantly — a decision that left it fatally vulnerable. The New Republic was largely destroyed when Starkiller Base obliterated the Hosnian system (its capital at the time) in *The Force Awakens*.

### 5.7 The First Order (c. 21 ABY – 35 ABY)

A military junta that grew from Imperial remnants who fled to the Unknown Regions after the Empire's collapse. Led by Supreme Leader Snoke (secretly a puppet of the resurrected Palpatine), it rebuilt the Imperial war machine in secret, complete with a new generation of stormtroopers (children kidnapped and indoctrinated from birth — Finn's origin). After Snoke's death, Kylo Ren became Supreme Leader.

### 5.8 The Resistance (c. 29 ABY – 35 ABY)

A paramilitary organization founded by General Leia Organa when the New Republic refused to take the First Order threat seriously. Smaller and less resourced than the old Rebel Alliance, the Resistance fought a desperate holding action until rallying the galaxy at the Battle of Exegol.

### 5.9 The Mandalorians

Not a species but a culture — Mandalorians are warriors from the planet Mandalore who follow a creed and wear distinctive beskar (Mandalorian iron) armor. Their history is one of constant warfare, both external and civil. Key groups include:
- **Death Watch** — A traditionalist militant faction
- **The Children of the Watch** — A strict orthodox sect (Din Djarin's covert)
- **The Nite Owls** — Bo-Katan Kryze's loyalist faction
- The **Darksaber** — an ancient black-bladed lightsaber created by the first Mandalorian Jedi, Tarre Vizsla. Possessing it is a traditional claim to the title of Mandalore's ruler.

### 5.10 Criminal Underworld

- **The Hutt Cartel** — Dominated Outer Rim crime for centuries (Jabba, the Hutt Council)
- **Black Sun** — A powerful crime syndicate
- **Crimson Dawn** — Run by Maul from the shadows, with Qi'ra as his lieutenant
- **The Pyke Syndicate** — Major spice (drug) traffickers
- **Bounty Hunters' Guild** — An association of hunters-for-hire (Boba Fett, Din Djarin, Bossk, IG-88, Dengar)

## 6. Technology

### 6.1 Starships

Star Wars is defined by its iconic starships:

**Fighters:**
- X-wing — The Rebellion/Resistance's primary starfighter
- TIE Fighter — The Empire's mass-produced fighter (no shields, no hyperdrive in the basic model)
- A-wing — Fast Rebel interceptor
- Y-wing — Rebel bomber
- Jedi Starfighter (Delta-7, Eta-2) — Small, agile fighters used by Jedi in the Clone Wars
- TIE Interceptor, TIE Defender, TIE Bomber — Imperial variants

**Freighters and transports:**
- The Millennium Falcon — A modified YT-1300 Corellian freighter, the fastest ship in the galaxy (Han Solo and Chewbacca's ship)
- The Razor Crest — Din Djarin's gunship (destroyed on Tython)
- The Ghost — The crew's ship in *Rebels*

**Capital ships:**
- Imperial Star Destroyer — 1.6 km long, the symbol of Imperial power
- Super Star Destroyer (Executor-class) — Darth Vader's flagship, 19 km long
- Mon Calamari cruisers — The backbone of the Rebel fleet
- Venator-class Star Destroyer — Republic warship during the Clone Wars
- Resurgent-class Star Destroyer — First Order's upgraded model

**Superweapons:**
- Death Star I and II — Moon-sized battle stations with planet-killing superlasers
- Starkiller Base — A planet (Ilum) converted into a sun-powered weapon capable of destroying entire star systems
- The Xyston-class Star Destroyers — Each ship in Palpatine's Final Order fleet carried a planet-killing cannon

### 6.2 Droids

Droids are ubiquitous in the Star Wars galaxy — artificial beings that range from simple tools to near-sentient companions. Despite their intelligence and personality, droids are treated as property, which creates an uncomfortable undercurrent in the saga.

- **Astromech droids** — R2-D2, BB-8, Chopper — small, versatile droids that assist with navigation, repairs, and hacking
- **Protocol droids** — C-3PO — designed for translation and etiquette (fluent in over six million forms of communication)
- **Battle droids** — The Separatists' expendable army (B1, B2 super battle droids, Droidekas)
- **Assassin droids** — IG-88, IG-11, HK-47 (Legends)
- **Medical droids** — 2-1B surgical droids
- **K-2SO** — A reprogrammed Imperial security droid who fought for the Rebellion

### 6.3 Weapons

- **Blasters** — The standard ranged weapon of the galaxy, firing bolts of plasma energy
- **Lightsabers** — Plasma blades powered by kyber crystals, wielded almost exclusively by Force users. Red blades are created by "bleeding" a kyber crystal through the dark side.
- **Bowcaster** — The Wookiee crossbow-like energy weapon (Chewbacca's signature)
- **Vibroblade** — A melee weapon with a vibrating blade that can resist lightsabers
- **The Darksaber** — A unique flat-bladed, black lightsaber of Mandalorian origin
- **Beskar** — Mandalorian iron, one of the few materials that can withstand lightsaber strikes

## 7. The Saga Timeline

Star Wars dates are measured relative to the Battle of Yavin (the destruction of the first Death Star in *A New Hope*): **BBY** = Before the Battle of Yavin, **ABY** = After the Battle of Yavin.

### 7.1 The Ancient Era (thousands of years BBY)

- The Jedi Order is founded on Ahch-To (the Prime Jedi)
- The Sith split from the Jedi, leading to millennia of war
- Darth Bane establishes the Rule of Two (c. 1,000 BBY)
- The Republic is founded and grows to encompass most of the galaxy
- The Mandalorian-Jedi wars rage across centuries

### 7.2 The Prequel Era (32–19 BBY)

**Episode I — The Phantom Menace (32 BBY):** The Trade Federation blockades Naboo. Qui-Gon Jinn discovers Anakin Skywalker, a slave boy on Tatooine with unprecedented Force potential. Darth Maul reveals the Sith have returned. Qui-Gon dies; Obi-Wan takes Anakin as his Padawan. Palpatine becomes Supreme Chancellor.

**Episode II — Attack of the Clones (22 BBY):** A Separatist movement threatens the Republic. Obi-Wan discovers a secret clone army on Kamino. Anakin and Padmé Amidala fall in love. The Clone Wars begin at the Battle of Geonosis.

**The Clone Wars (22–19 BBY):** A galaxy-wide conflict between the Republic (with its clone army) and the Separatists (with their droid army). Both sides are secretly controlled by Palpatine. During this period, Anakin trains Ahsoka Tano, who later leaves the Jedi Order. The war deepens Anakin's disillusionment with the Jedi.

**Episode III — Revenge of the Sith (19 BBY):** Palpatine reveals himself as Darth Sidious and turns Anakin to the dark side (as Darth Vader) by promising to save Padmé from death. Palpatine executes **Order 66**, a command embedded in the clones that forces them to kill their Jedi generals. The Jedi are nearly wiped out. Palpatine declares himself Emperor. Obi-Wan defeats Anakin on Mustafar, leaving him burned and broken. Anakin is rebuilt as the armored Darth Vader. Padmé dies giving birth to twins — Luke and Leia — who are hidden from the Empire.

### 7.3 The Imperial Era (19 BBY – 0 BBY)

- The Empire rises. Jedi survivors go into hiding.
- The Inquisitors hunt remaining Jedi.
- Resistance cells begin forming, eventually uniting into the Rebel Alliance.
- *Rebels* (14–0 BBY), *Andor* (5 BBY), *Obi-Wan Kenobi* (9 BBY), and *Rogue One* (0 BBY) take place during this period.

### 7.4 The Original Trilogy Era (0–4 ABY)

**Episode IV — A New Hope (0 BBY/ABY):** Luke Skywalker leaves Tatooine, joins the Rebellion, rescues Princess Leia, and destroys the Death Star. Obi-Wan is killed by Vader but becomes one with the Force.

**Episode V — The Empire Strikes Back (3 ABY):** The Empire strikes back at Hoth. Luke trains with Yoda on Dagobah. Han is frozen in carbonite. Vader reveals he is Luke's father.

**Episode VI — Return of the Jedi (4 ABY):** Luke confronts Vader and the Emperor. Vader, moved by Luke's love, turns on the Emperor and kills him — fulfilling the prophecy of the Chosen One. The second Death Star is destroyed at Endor. The Empire begins to crumble.

### 7.5 The New Republic Era (5–28 ABY)

- The Battle of Jakku (5 ABY) delivers the final blow to the Empire, which surrenders.
- The New Republic is established. Imperial remnants flee to the Unknown Regions.
- Luke attempts to rebuild the Jedi Order.
- Ben Solo (son of Han and Leia) is corrupted by Snoke and destroys Luke's temple, becoming Kylo Ren.
- *The Mandalorian* (9 ABY), *The Book of Boba Fett* (9 ABY), and *Ahsoka* (c. 9 ABY) take place during this era.

### 7.6 The Sequel Era (34–35 ABY)

**Episode VII — The Force Awakens (34 ABY):** The First Order destroys the Hosnian system. Rey, Finn, and Poe join the Resistance. Han Solo is killed by his son Kylo Ren. Rey finds Luke Skywalker on Ahch-To.

**Episode VIII — The Last Jedi (34 ABY):** Luke trains Rey reluctantly. The Resistance is decimated. Luke projects himself across the galaxy to save the Resistance, then dies and becomes one with the Force. Rey and Kylo's connection deepens.

**Episode IX — The Rise of Skywalker (35 ABY):** Palpatine is revealed to have survived. Rey discovers she is Palpatine's granddaughter. Kylo Ren is redeemed and becomes Ben Solo again. Together, Rey and Ben (a Force dyad) defeat Palpatine. Ben gives his life to save Rey. The galaxy rises up against the Final Order. Rey takes the name Skywalker.

## 8. Themes and Philosophy

At its deepest level, Star Wars is about:

**Redemption** — The belief that no one is ever truly lost. Vader's return to the light is the emotional core of the saga.

**The tension between attachment and selflessness** — Anakin's fall was driven by his fear of losing those he loved. The Jedi's prohibition on attachment was both wise and tragically flawed.

**The corruption of power** — Democracy can die with thunderous applause. Institutions decay from within.

**Hope** — The Rebellion's greatest weapon is hope itself. Even in the darkest moments — the fall of the Republic, the destruction of Luke's academy, the decimation of the Resistance — hope endures.

**Legacy and choice** — You are not defined by your bloodline or origin. Rey, a Palpatine, chose the light. Luke, born of Vader, chose the light. Identity is a choice, not an inheritance.

**Balance** — The Force seeks balance. The saga's deepest mystery is what balance truly means — not the triumph of light over dark, but a deeper harmony.
